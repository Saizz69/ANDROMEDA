Scanning Preferences

Tune when and what TruthScan analyzes on your web pages.

Auto-Scan Active Pages
Automatically discovers and marks visible content as you scroll. If turned off, badges appear only when you hover over items.
Scan Text Posts & Article Snippets
Identifies paragraphs, social media posts, tweets, and news stories with factual statements.
Scan Embedded Images
Cross-references viral photos, deepfakes, and manipulated graphics against verified image records.
Scan Video Embeds
Monitors YouTube, Vimeo, and HTML5 video blocks for out-of-context audio or visual hoaxes.
Verification Sensitivity
Controls how strictly content is flagged as "Needs context" or "Likely misleading".
Low: Permissive, flags only high-probability debunked media. Medium: Balanced, highlights claims needing context. High: Strict, scrutinizes sensational language and unverified claims.

How TruthScan Works

Designed around transparency, user control, and zero layout disruption.

The Traffic-Light Rating Scale

90%+
Verified Accurate
Matches confirmed wire reports, scientific publications, or primary official releases. Shows clickable real source check.
50 - 75%
Needs Context
Partially factual but leaves out crucial nuance (e.g. inflation adjustments, selective quoting, correlation vs causation).
< 30%
Likely Misleading / Manipulated
Debunked by accredited fact-checkers, confirmed synthetic AI imagery, or known fabricated disinformation.
?
Unverified — No Source Data
No conclusive fact-check or primary source exists. TruthScan never fabricates an answer; it invites manual search.

Zero-Disruption Guarantee

TruthScan uses isolated Shadow DOM overlays. It never inserts blocking banners, never blurs your screen, and never shifts the page layout. The original content remains 100% visible and unobstructed.
